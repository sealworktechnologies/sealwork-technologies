RUBBER MATERIAL KNOWLEDGE

The right material for the right application.

Different applications require different rubber properties. The following are common elastomer families used in industrial rubber components. Final selection depends on operating conditions and technical specifications.

MATERIAL 01

Natural Rubber (NR)

Known for elasticity, resilience and abrasion resistance. Commonly considered for flexible components, cushioning and vibration-related applications.

MATERIAL 02

EPDM Rubber

Offers weathering, ozone and water resistance. Often used in outdoor sealing, automotive weather seals and water-related applications.

MATERIAL 03

NBR Rubber

Known for oil and fuel resistance in suitable grades. Commonly used for seals, gaskets and components exposed to petroleum-based fluids.

MATERIAL 04

Silicone Rubber

Available in grades designed for wide temperature performance. Used in selected electrical, industrial, food-contact and sealing applications.

MATERIAL 05

Neoprene (CR)

Offers a balance of weather, ozone and moderate oil resistance. Used in selected industrial seals, covers and flexible components.

MATERIAL 06

SBR Rubber

Commonly used where abrasion resistance and general-purpose rubber properties are important, depending on the formulation.

MATERIAL 07

FKM / Fluoroelastomer

Specialist elastomer family used in demanding chemical, oil and higher-temperature environments, subject to grade compatibility.

MATERIAL 08

Butyl Rubber (IIR)

Known for low gas permeability and good weather resistance. Used in selected sealing and insulation-related applications.

MATERIAL 09

Custom Rubber Compounds

Rubber formulations can be selected or developed around required hardness, flexibility, temperature and environmental conditions.

Material availability and processing capability must be confirmed against the actual manufacturing setup and customer specification.
