Rubber Material Guide

The Right Material For Every Application

Different Industrial Applications Require Different Rubber Properties. Explore Common Rubber Material Categories And Their Typical Uses.

NR

Natural Rubber

Known For Elasticity, Resilience And Abrasion Resistance. Used In Suitable Vibration And General Engineering Applications.

EP

EPDM Rubber

Offers Good Weather, Ozone, Water And Heat Resistance. Commonly Used In Outdoor And Automotive Sealing Applications.

NB

NBR Rubber

Known For Oil And Fuel Resistance. Used In Suitable Oil Seals, Gaskets And Industrial Applications.

SI

Silicone Rubber

Offers Wide Temperature Flexibility And Weather Resistance. Used In Suitable Electrical And Industrial Applications.

CR

Neoprene Rubber

Provides A Balance Of Weather, Oil And Mechanical Properties For Selected Industrial Applications.

SB

SBR Rubber

Commonly Used Where Good Abrasion Resistance And General Mechanical Properties Are Required.

FK

FKM Rubber

Designed For Demanding Applications Requiring High Temperature And Chemical Resistance.

BU

Butyl Rubber

Known For Low Gas Permeability And Good Resistance To Weathering In Suitable Applications.

CU

Custom Rubber Compounds

Special Compound Selection Can Be Considered Based On Hardness, Temperature, Fluid And Performance Requirements.

Material Suitability Depends On The Actual Operating Environment, Temperature, Fluid Exposure, Hardness, Design And Applicable Standards. Please Confirm Material Availability And Specifications With Our Team.
